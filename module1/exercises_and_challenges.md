## Exercise 1: Fill-in-the-pattern
Complete the following prompt templates by filling in the blanks with appropriate content.

### 1.1: Email Writing
**Template:**
```
Write a [FORMALITY] email to [AUDIENCE] about [TOPIC]. The email should be [LENGTH] and include [KEY_ELEMENTS].
```

**Complete the template to write a formal email to a potential client about a new product launch.**

### 1.2: Story Generation
**Template:**
```

```

**Complete the template to write a mystery story featuring a detective who loses their memory.**

### 1.3: Technical Explanation
**Template:**
```

```

**Complete the template to explain blockchain technology to a beginner.**

## Evaluation Criteria
Your fixed prompts will be evaluated on:
1. **Clarity**: Is the request clear and unambiguous?
2. **Specificity**: Does it provide enough detail for good output?
3. **Feasibility**: Are the constraints achievable?
4. **Context**: Does it provide necessary background information?
