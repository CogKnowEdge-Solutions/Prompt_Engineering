# Group 6: Testing & Maintaining Prompts

**Modules:** 9, 10

Stop assuming a prompt works. Module 9 is how you prove it — golden datasets, rubric scoring, LLM-as-a-judge. Module 10 is what you do with the evidence — prompts as versioned artifacts, staged deployment, a real iteration loop.

| Order | Module | Topic | Prerequisite |
|-------|--------|-------|--------------|
| 1st | [Module 9: Prompt Evaluation](./module9/) | Golden datasets, LLM-as-a-judge, A/B testing, production pipelines | Module 6 |
| 2nd | [Module 10: Iteration Workflow](./module10/) | Versioned prompts, staged deployment, the practical iteration loop | Modules 8 and 9 |

**Time:** ~85-95 min of lesson reading, plus labs and exercises.
